Privacy Policy

Starter policy — review and adapt before public launch.

Information we collect

Lock-in (Student Internship & Skill Tracker) stores account details you submit, including name, email address and password hash, plus profile details, skills, projects, courses and internship/application records you enter.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account and provide the tracker features. Passwords are stored as hashes, not as readable passwords. Do not enter sensitive personal information into profile fields.

Storage and security

Account and application data are stored in the configured MongoDB database. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Access to the database should be restricted and credentials kept private.

Direct messages

The current direct-message feature is not end-to-end encrypted. Message text is processed by the application server and stored in the configured database. HTTPS protects traffic in transit when the production HTTPS configuration is correctly deployed, and database-provider encryption-at-rest settings protect underlying storage, but authorized service/database operators may be able to access message content. The service also processes conversation participants and timestamps. Do not send highly sensitive information through direct messages.

Transport and hosting

Production should use HTTPS for all public traffic and TLS for database connections. Database backups and retention depend on the configured hosting and database-provider settings. The operator must restrict database access, maintain backups securely, and configure retention and deletion practices.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your account information or request help. The current version does not promise automated data export or deletion tools.
